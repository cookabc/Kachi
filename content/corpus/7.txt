The euro came under pressure as a selloff in French government bonds drove the country's debt risk premium to its highest since the sovereign debt crisis. The currency fell against the dollar and the yen. Traders said the exchange rate reflected a widening budget deficit and the risk of a rating downgrade. A weaker euro would make imports dearer and could push inflation higher. The dollar index, which measures the greenback against a basket of currencies, was little changed. Analysts expect the euro to depreciate further if the fiscal deficit keeps widening, though a hawkish central bank could see it appreciate instead.
